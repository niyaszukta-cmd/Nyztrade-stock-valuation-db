Five ways to value the same company

No single method suits every business. Run one on its own, or all five blended into a weighted fair value — and see where they disagree.

1Your P/E

What the earnings are worth, on a multiple you choose.

EPS × target P/E

Breaks down on negative or cyclical earnings.

2Industry P/E

The same arithmetic, on the multiple the peer group trades at.

EPS × peer P/E

Inherits the mood of the sector, good or bad.

3Price / Book

Net assets per share, repriced at a multiple.

BVPS × target P/B

Built for banks and NBFCs. Weak for asset-light firms.

4EV / EBITDA

Values the whole business, then nets off debt to leave equity.

(EBITDA × mult − net debt) ÷ shares

Ignores capex, so it flatters heavy spenders.

5Graham Number

A deliberately strict floor from earnings and book together.

√(22.5 × EPS × BVPS)

Will call almost any growth company expensive.

★All five, blended

A weighted fair value across every method that has the inputs it needs, with the spread between them shown rather than hidden.

30 / 25 / 20 / 17 / 8 weighting
